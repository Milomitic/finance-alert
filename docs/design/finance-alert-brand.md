## Prompt originale

Use case: logo-brand. Create one final standalone symbol/logo for Finance Alert, an Italian stock-market monitoring and research web application. This is the production logo asset for its sign-in page, not a mockup. Brand context: restrained dark financial workstation, charcoal backgrounds, existing emerald green accent #1ed760. Design a distinctive, simple geometric monogram that subtly combines F and A with a single small signal/observation dot. Confident balanced proportions, precise flat vector-like geometry, generous negative space, memorable silhouette readable at 40px. The meaning is observation and attention, not guaranteed growth. Only emerald-green solid shapes, with at most a lighter green accent. Truly transparent background, square composition, logo centered filling about 80% of the canvas. No wording, no letters outside the monogram, no wordmark, no chart, no rising arrow, no bell cliché, no gradients, no 3D, no glow, no drop shadow, no texture, no border or app-icon tile, no presentation sheet. Produce only the clean finished logo mark.
